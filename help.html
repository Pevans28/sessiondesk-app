Help & data safety

SessionDesk is a lightweight client and prepaid-session tracker. Use it to track packages, schedule appointments and manually record payments.

Does it charge clients?

No. Recording a payment is bookkeeping only. The app does not process your customers' payments or automatically issue invoices.

Will my data sync across devices?

Local mode stores client records inside this browser. Download a JSON backup regularly in Settings. Cloud mode requires a separately configured account and database. Until enabled, local data does not sync.

What if I clear my browser?

Clearing site data or switching browsers can permanently erase local records. Export a backup first and restore it on the new device.

Does the calendar send invitations?

No, the calendar tracks appointments locally or in your signed-in cloud workspace. Reminders can be copied manually.

Can I put health or financial information in notes?

No. Avoid storing sensitive medical, government identification or financial data in this starter product. Production privacy/security reviews are still required before commercial launch.

How do I install the app?

On supported browsers use Install App or Add to Home Screen from your browser menu. The local interface can work offline after the page has cached; cloud syncing and online billing require internet access.
